Generated by `python -m futuresres.data.splice`. CLAUDE_FUTURES.md §3.

NQ through **2019-05-31**, MNQ after. MNQ began trading 2019-05-06, so the two overlap for most of May 2019 and the convention can be checked before anything is joined.

## The convention check

Overlap window **2019-05-01 to 2019-05-31**, comparing closes on minutes where both series have a bar.

| | |
|---|---|
| matched minutes | **25,657** |
| median close ratio MNQ/NQ | **1.000000000** |
| ratio range | 0.997640 to 1.002847 |
| median absolute difference | **0.2500 index points** |
| 99th percentile difference | 1.7500 |
| maximum difference | 22.0000 |
| within one tick (0.25) | **63.36%** |
| exactly identical | 6,869 (26.8%) |

**PASS — identical index points, no scaling.** The median ratio is 1 to nine decimal places and the full range stays inside 1%. MNQ is one tenth the NOTIONAL of NQ, not one tenth the price, so the series concatenate directly.

Residual differences are microstructure, not convention: NQ and MNQ are separate books with separate order flow, so a minute's close is the last trade in each book and the books do not trade in lockstep. That is why the check asserts the RATIO and reports the difference distribution, rather than requiring bit-identical prints.
