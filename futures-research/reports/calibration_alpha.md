# Stage 1 bootstrap α — re-measured on futures

Generated by `python -m futuresres.integrity.calibration --part alpha`. CLAUDE_FUTURES.md §4, §5 Stage 1.

2,000 replications per cell. Each draws a **contiguous window of real returns**, so the tail and the serial dependence are the market's own rather than a model's. The statistic is the signed shift `evaluate_signed_signal` computes, block-bootstrapped with the same 240-bar blocks.

Monte Carlo error on a coverage near 95% at 2,000 reps is about ±0.96 points.
