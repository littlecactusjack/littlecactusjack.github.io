Read: the plan is a fast, high-variance staking game; it pays where payouts are frequent and **no consistency rule caps the best day**.
Consistency rules (MFF's 30% evaluation, Phidias's 30% funded) or slow payout cycles destroy it. Tradeify's Select Daily — the cheapest
evaluation, 40% evaluation consistency only, daily payouts, and the only US-friendly firm with FDXM — remains best even after its live rules.
Uncapped daily-payout firms (TPT, Lucid) have higher means only when every slot is NKD, and with ~35–45% of runs losing money.
