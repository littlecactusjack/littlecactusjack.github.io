# Placebo matching

Generated by `python -m futuresres.reporting.level_rates`. LEVEL_HYPOTHESES.md, methodological core.

**Every L-series result reports (real - placebo), not (real - 0).** Price reacts at arbitrary levels, so a raw reaction statistic is not evidence that a level matters. This document is the evidence that the placebo comparison is valid at all.

Offsets are SHA-256 of (session date, level type, index within session), mapped to +/-[0.3, 1.5] x the INTRADAY RANGE OVER EACH LEVEL'S OWN VALIDITY WINDOW. Deterministic across processes - never Python's salted `hash()`, which is the bug that forced a floor sweep to be discarded and re-run earlier in this project.

**The scale was daily ATR(20) until 2026-09-09 and that failed 53 of 55 level types** - placebos sat 3x to 63x further from price than the real levels they stood in for, so every comparison would have measured exposure rather than reaction. The offset BOUNDS are unchanged; only the unit they multiply. See `decisions.md` 36.
