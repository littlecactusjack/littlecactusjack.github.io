# Kurtosis and the DSR event floor

Generated by `python -m futuresres.reporting.kurtosis`. CLAUDE_FUTURES.md §4, §7.

Non-excess kurtosis (3 for a normal), measured on front-month continuous series. Returns are taken **within one contract and one session**, with endpoints exactly h minutes apart, so none spans a roll, the maintenance break or a weekend.

**n_min = 1 + (z/2)² · (γ₄ − 1)**, with z = Φ⁻¹(0.95) = 1.6449. Below n_min the Deflated Sharpe cannot reach 0.95 at ANY effect size — the estimator's own standard error swamps it.

## The prediction was wrong, and it is worth being precise about how

`FUTURES_STRATEGY_HYPOTHESES.md` states that index futures run "an order of magnitude lower" than BTC's kurtosis and that the 46-event wall "drops to roughly 5 events". Measured:

- **MGC at one minute is γ₄ = 226.5 — FATTER-TAILED THAN BITCOIN (199.9).** Gold's one-minute tail is not milder than crypto's; it is worse, with a skew of -1.47 that says the weight is on the downside.
- MNQ at one minute is 115.1 against BTC's 199.9 — lower, but by a factor of 1.7, not ten.
- The improvement is real but it lives at LONGER horizons, where aggregation thins the tail. That is where the useful part of the prediction survives.

The direction of the error matters more than its size. The catalog treated a lower floor as a reason to expect more hypotheses to be provable; at one minute the floor is *higher* than crypto's for gold. Anything operating on one-minute bars in MGC inherits that.

## Against BTC

| | BTC (crypto project) | MNQ | MGC |
|---|---|---|---|
| kurtosis at 1m | **199.9** | 115.1 | 226.5 |
| kurtosis at 60m | **66.9** | 25.9 | 25.6 |
| n_min at 1m | **136** | **79** | **154** |
| n_min at 60m | **46** | **18** | **18** |

**The wall moves, but not to where the catalog said.** At BTC's 60-minute kurtosis of 66.9 the floor is 46 events. At the measured futures values it is **18 for MNQ** and **18 for MGC** — a genuine 2.6x improvement, against a predicted tenfold one. 'Roughly 5 events' is not what the data says at any horizon measured here; the lowest floor found is 10 at 180 minutes.

This removes a constraint, it does not create an edge. The floor that closed hypotheses in the crypto catalog was the FIRING-RATE floor of ~2,100 events, which comes from effect size against volatility and is untouched by kurtosis. What changes is that a rare-but-large effect is no longer unprovable on principle.

## Caveat

These are unconditional, full-sample moments. Kurtosis is not stationary — it rises in crisis and falls in calm — so a hypothesis concentrated in one regime faces that regime's tail, not this average. The §5 Stage 3 regime split is where that gets checked; this table sets the pipeline's calibration, not any hypothesis's floor.
