Verdict

No edge accessible at this cost structure and account size was found in intraday, calendar or non-price futures signals, across six independently designed series.

  • Cost binds. A round trip on MNQ costs 0.48 bps. The largest effect measured anywhere was +0.452 bps (NQ/ES relative value), so no trade frequency or size can make it pay.
  • Sample binds. Once-a-session mechanisms give about 4,000 events against about 19,722 needed to detect them.
  • Account rules bind. Flat by 17:00 ET and no correlated long/short ruled out overnight holds and the strongest unexplored idea (a cross-sectional book).
  • Reopening needs new information (order-flow or book data), different account permissions, lower cost, or years of new sessions, not a seventh series on the same 1-minute bars.

How it compares to the old tests

    Full grid

    Columns are the evaluation's daily dollar volatility (one MNQ held through the session is about $850/day). Each cell: EV per evaluation, the pass rate, and the funded-account size that maximises payout, chosen separately.

    Not in the model

    • Anything a Live account pays after the $5k Express cap (excluded, so EV is understated there).
    • The firm refusing payouts on accounts it judges were gamed. This is the largest real risk, and no simulation prices it.
    • Terms read from your description and third-party sites, since topstep.com was unreachable: the per-payout cap ($2k reported for accounts without a daily loss limit, $5k headline; both run), the payout rule (50% of balance as published, or above a $2k buffer as you described; both run) and whether the $90 renews monthly (both run).
    • The funded scaling plan (2, 3 then 5 lots by balance). It is in full-size contracts, far above every size here, so it never binds.
